Bluetooth Finder for Headsets

BT Finder for iPhone

BT Finder listens for Bluetooth signals from nearby headphones, earbuds and tracker tags and turns their signal strength into a hot-and-cold meter, so you can walk toward the one you've misplaced.

It can only detect devices that are switched on, have battery left and are within Bluetooth range. Everything happens on your iPhone: no account, no server, no tracking.
