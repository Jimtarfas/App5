What EmberSeek can and can't do

EmberSeek estimates how strong a nearby device's Bluetooth signal is. It can only detect devices that are powered on, broadcasting Bluetooth and within range. It cannot find devices that are switched off, out of battery or out of range, it does not show locations on a map, and signal strength is affected by walls, bags and people. EmberSeek is a convenience tool and is provided “as is”, without any guarantee that a device will be found.

Acceptable use

Use EmberSeek only to look for your own devices or devices you have permission to locate. Don't use it to track people.
