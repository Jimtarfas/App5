Bluetooth Finder for Headsets (“BT Finder”)

Need help? Email nouhe.tarfas@gmail.com and we'll reply within two business days.

My headphones or earbuds don't show up

BT Finder can only hear devices that are switched on and broadcasting Bluetooth. Many earbuds only broadcast when they're out of the case or the case lid is open, and some stop broadcasting while they're connected to a phone. Try opening the case, or disconnecting the device in iOS Settings → Bluetooth, then scan again. Also check Settings → Nearby list → Show unnamed devices, because some devices don't broadcast a name.

Can BT Finder find a device that is off or out of battery?

No. A device that is switched off, out of battery or out of range sends no Bluetooth signal, so no app can detect it. Saved devices show when your iPhone last heard them, which can help you remember where they were.

How does the hot/cold meter work?

It shows the device's Bluetooth signal strength. A stronger signal usually means you're closer. Walls, bags and your body weaken the signal, so walk slowly, turn around and follow the trend rather than single jumps.

Why did a saved device appear as a new device?

Some devices change their Bluetooth identity regularly for privacy. When that happens, iOS reports them as a new device.

Does it scan in the background?

No. BT Finder scans only while the app is open.

How do I restore my subscription?

Open BT Finder → Settings → Restore Purchases, or tap Restore Purchases on the Pro screen. Use the same Apple Account you subscribed with.

How do I cancel?

On your iPhone open Settings → your name → Subscriptions → BT Finder, then Cancel Subscription. You can also tap Manage Subscription in BT Finder's Settings.
